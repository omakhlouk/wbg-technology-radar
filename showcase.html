Made to be watched

The three-minute sequence highlights 63 builders across 12 trend scenes and five company close-ups. Sarvam AI, SatSure, Factory, Reducto and LILA Sciences receive a closer look at their capabilities and potential WBG relevance. The wider radar contains 102 profiles; names rotate so they can be read without shrinking the type.

WBG references and branding

Each scene names documented WBG work. The company applications are proposed connections, with the supporting institutional and company sources available in Scene insights. Selected evidence was refreshed on 24 November 2026; the broader research baseline is 23 November.

The official WBG vector mark retains its original logotype. The films use Arial Regular and Bold, the WBG secondary typeface, with corporate blue accents and protected logo clear space.
